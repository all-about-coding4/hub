Features

Everything You Need to Manage Apps

LynkHub gives you full control over your app ecosystem — from discovery to testing and beyond.

Upload from GitHub

Submit your app with a GitHub project URL, token, and description. Perfect for developers who want to share their work.

Add by URL

Don't have a GitHub project? Just paste any app URL and LynkHub will add it to your store instantly.

Phone‑Like Home Screen

Installed apps appear as beautiful icons on a home screen. Tap to launch, long‑press to uninstall.

Sandbox Testing

Launch any installed app in a safe, isolated iframe environment. Test without risk to your main system.

Share & Discover

Share any app via a unique link. Others can view and install it directly from the shared URL.

4 Premium Themes

Choose from Light, Dark, Ocean, and Forest themes. The entire UI adapts smoothly to your preference.

How It Works

Get Started in 4 Simple Steps

From uploading to launching — here's how LynkHub transforms your app experience.

1

Upload or Add

Submit your app via GitHub (project URL + token) or add any app by its URL. Include a description to help others discover it.

2

Browse & Install

Explore the App Store, view app details, and install with a single tap. Grant permissions just like on a real phone.

3

Launch & Test

Installed apps appear on your Home screen. Tap any icon to launch it in a full‑screen sandbox environment.

4

Manage & Share

Uninstall with a tap or long‑press. Share apps via unique links so others can discover and install them too.

Data & Privacy

Your Data, Your Control

LynkHub is designed with transparency in mind. Here's exactly what data we handle and how it's used.

GitHub Project URL

Used to fetch your app's source and metadata. Stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

GitHub Token

Used for authentication when accessing private repositories. Never transmitted — stored only in your browser.

App Metadata

Name, description, category, and icon. All stored locally to build your personalized app store.

Installation Status

Which apps you've installed. Saved locally to remember your preferences across sessions.

Theme Preference

Your chosen theme (Light, Dark, Ocean, Forest) is stored locally for a consistent experience.

No Cloud Storage

All data stays in your browser. LynkHub does not send any data to external servers — it's fully client‑side.
